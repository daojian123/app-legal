Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026

1. Introduction

TinyForge ("we", "us" or "our") operates the LifeProgressBar mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information when you use it. By downloading or using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. The Short Version

The App is a fully offline, serverless application. We do not operate any backend servers, and we do not collect, transmit, store, sell or share your personal data. Everything you create or configure in the App stays on your device.

3. Information We Do Not Collect

The App is designed to work without collecting any information from you:

  • No account or registration is required to use the App.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos or any other personal information.
  • We do not use analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services that send data to us or to third parties.
  • We do not collect advertising identifiers or build user profiles.
  • No usage data, content or settings are ever transmitted from your device to us.

4. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

To provide its functionality, the App stores a limited amount of data locally on your device, such as:

  • Your preferences and settings within the App;
  • Content you create or enter while using the App.

This data is kept in the App's private storage area, is used only to make the App work, and is never uploaded or shared. We have no access to it. You can delete this data at any time by clearing the App's storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the App. Once deleted, it cannot be recovered by us.

5. Permissions

If the App requests any device permissions, they are used solely to provide local features on your device. Permissions are never used to collect or transmit data off your device.

6. Third-Party Services

The App is distributed through Google Play. When you download, update or purchase the App, Google may process certain data (such as device information and purchase records) in accordance with the Google Privacy Policy. We do not control and are not responsible for Google's data practices.

Other than the distribution platform described above, the App does not integrate any third-party services that collect data from you.

7. In-App Purchases

The App may offer optional in-app purchases. All payments are processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We never receive, process or store your payment card details or other financial information. The App only stores the resulting purchase status locally on your device in order to unlock the corresponding features. Purchase records held by Google are governed by Google's own privacy policy.

8. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in the relevant jurisdiction). Since the App collects no personal data at all, no such information is held by us. If you believe a child has provided personal information through some other channel, please contact us and we will promptly address it.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Because we do not collect or store any personal data on servers, there is no data for us to retain or delete. All App data lives on your device and is fully under your control: you can remove it at any time by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling the App.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct, export or delete your personal data. Since we hold no personal data about you on any server, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete. Any data created in the App can be managed or deleted by you directly on your device. If you have any privacy-related request, you can still contact us at the address below.

11. Security

Your data remains within the App's private storage, protected by the operating system's application sandbox. Because no data is transmitted over the network, there is no risk of interception in transit. Please note that no method of electronic storage is 100% secure, and the security of your device itself (screen lock, OS updates, etc.) is your responsibility.
